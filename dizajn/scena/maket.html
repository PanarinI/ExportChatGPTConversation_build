Plan a 3-day trip to Lisbon for two, mid-range budget.

A relaxed pace: one big sight each morning, free evenings. Day 1 — Alfama walk, São Jorge Castle, a fado dinner. Day 2 — Belém Tower, the Jerónimos Monastery and pastéis de Belém. Day 3 — the train to Sintra and the Pena Palace.

What should we pack for early October?

Layers: days around 22 °C, cooler evenings by the river. Comfortable shoes — Lisbon is hilly and paved with slippery stone.
