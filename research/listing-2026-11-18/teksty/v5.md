📦 ChatGPT export, chat by chat
Looking for how to export a chat from ChatGPT as a clean, readable file? This chatgpt export extension takes one whole thread, every question and every answer, and turns it into PDF or Word. Or it hands everything to another app, so you can go on working there.
It does not pull the archive of your entire account: you get the thread that is open in front of you.

⚡ How it works
1️⃣ Go to the dialogue you want to keep
2️⃣ Click the arrow on the extension's button at the top of the page
3️⃣ Pick PDF, Word or Claude
4️⃣ Press the button, then save the file or paste the text

📄 PDF with real pages
If you wonder how to export chatgpt conversation to pdf without screenshots and copy-paste, pick PDF. The pages are set for reading and printing:
➤ contents and bookmarks, one per question
➤ page numbers, calm margins and page breaks
➤ formulas, tables and code the way you see them on screen
➤ pictures from your questions and from the answers
➤ a light sheet by default, a dark one if you like

📝 Word and Google Docs
Need to know how to download chatgpt conversation as an editable file? As a chatgpt to word extension, it builds the .docx right in your browser. Headings, lists, tables, code, links, pictures and formulas arrive as Word's own, ready to edit.
Prefer Google Docs? Choose it under the arrow on the Word tile. Everything is copied with its formatting and a new document opens, so all that is left is to paste. That makes it a simple chatgpt to google docs extension. Pictures stay in the Word file; in Docs a short note marks where each one was.

🤖 Go on in another app
Want another assistant to go on with the same thread? Choose Claude: every question and answer is copied with one line on top, and claude.ai opens for you to paste it.
ChatGPT to Notion works the same way: a new Notion page opens, and the text is waiting in your clipboard. For Obsidian, a new note with the whole text appears in your vault by itself.

📋 Plain text for any editor
Wondering how to export chatgpt conversation as text? Choose Markdown file: plain text with headings that opens anywhere. A ChatGPT to Markdown extension is handy for notes, wikis and other AI tools. Instead of carrying pictures, it marks where each picture was.

✂️ Whole chat or only what matters
Not sure how to export chatgpt chat without the small talk around it? Under What goes in, keep Whole chat or choose Pick blocks and tick the questions and answers you want. Long threads are read from the first message to the last before the file is made, and you see how many messages have been read.

⚙️ Settings with a live preview
1. Three ready styles: Chat, Document and Compact
2. Font, text size and line spacing
3. Questions as bubbles, as headings, or hidden for answers only
4. Paper size, margins, page breaks or one long page
5. A header with the title, the date and a link back to the source
The first page of your file is drawn next to the settings and changes as you click.

💡 How to save chatgpt chats you will need later

• keep code answers with their indentation
• move a draft into Word or Docs to finish it

🔒 What leaves your computer
Word and Markdown files, and the copies for other apps, are made right in your browser. A PDF is made on our print server: the chat is sent there to build the pages and comes back as a file. No account and no sign-up.
