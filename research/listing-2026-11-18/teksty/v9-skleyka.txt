Export ChatGPT Conversation
Easily export ChatGPT conversation in one click: as a PDF or Word file, only the blocks you need, or straight to Claude.
📦 Export a conversation as a tidy file
The extension takes the dialogue in front of you, every question and every answer, and turns it into a PDF or a Word document. It can also hand everything on to Claude, Notion or Obsidian, or save it as Markdown.

⚡ How it works
1️⃣ Go to the dialogue you want to keep
2️⃣ Click the arrow on the extension's button at the top of the screen
3️⃣ Pick PDF, Word or Claude
4️⃣ Press the button, then save the file or paste the text
Next time, the main button goes straight to your last choice.

📄 A PDF with real pages
➤ contents and a bookmark for every question
➤ page numbers, calm margins and clean breaks
➤ formulas, tables and code as you see them on screen
➤ your own pictures and those in the answers
➤ a light sheet by default, or a dark sheet if you like

📝 Word and Google Docs
The .docx is built right in your browser. Headings, lists, tables, code, links, pictures and formulas arrive ready to edit.
For Google Docs, choose it under the arrow on the Word tile: everything is copied with its formatting, and a blank document appears for you to paste into. Pictures stay in the .docx; in Docs a short note marks where each picture was.

🤖 Go on in another app
Choose Claude: the whole dialogue is copied with a short line on top, and claude.ai opens for you to paste it. Notion works the same way: a new Notion page opens, and the text waits in your clipboard. For Obsidian, a new note with all of it appears in your vault by itself.

📋 Markdown for any editor
Plain text with headings that works anywhere: notes, wikis, other AI tools. Instead of carrying pictures, it marks where they were.

✂️ Whole chat or only what matters
Under What goes in, keep Whole chat or choose Pick blocks and tick the questions and answers you want. Long threads are read from the first message to the last before anything is made, and you see how many messages have been read.

⚙️ Settings with a live preview
1. Three ready styles: Chat, Document and Compact
2. Font, text size and line spacing
3. Questions as bubbles, as headings, or hidden for answers only
4. Paper size and margins; each question on its own sheet, or one long sheet
5. A header with the title, the date and a link back to the source
The first page is drawn next to the settings and changes as you click.

💡 Where it helps
• keep a long research thread as a file you can search
• print a conversation for a meeting or a lesson
• send a colleague the whole thread at once
• keep code answers with their indentation
• finish a draft in Google Docs or another editor

🔒 What leaves your computer
The .docx, the Markdown file and the copies for other apps are made right in your browser. A PDF is made on our print server: the conversation is sent there to be laid out and comes back to you.

❓ Good to know
📌 Can it save all my chats at once?
💡 No, it saves one dialogue at a time. The built-in data download of your ChatGPT account sends the full history as a single ZIP archive.
📌 Do I need an account?
💡 No account and no sign-up: install it, and the button appears at the top of the screen.
📌 Does it work in other languages?
💡 Yes. The pages carry fonts for Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Thai, Chinese, Japanese and many other scripts.
